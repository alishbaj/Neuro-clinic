Attorneys, physicians & organizations

Professional inquiry

For medical record review, case consultation, peer review, expert consultation, deposition or trial testimony, please contact us with a brief description of the matter, the jurisdiction, relevant deadlines and the nature of the engagement. We will respond regarding availability, conflicts, scope and fees.

Professional and medical-legal consulting is entirely separate from patient care. Please do not send patient records until an engagement has been confirmed in writing.

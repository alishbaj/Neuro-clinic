Hours

Virtual visits are offered on weekdays, with some early-morning and evening times for people who work. Available times are shown live in the scheduler.

Service area

Patients must be physically located in Texas at the time of a video visit. Educational family sessions and professional services are not restricted by patient location.

!

Sudden weakness or numbness, trouble speaking, facial drooping, loss of vision, the worst headache of your life, or a first seizure are emergencies. Call 911 — a virtual visit is not the right door.

Attorneys, physicians & organizations

Professional inquiry

For medical record review, case consultation, peer review, expert consultation, deposition or trial testimony, please contact us with a brief description of the matter, the jurisdiction, relevant deadlines and the nature of the engagement. We will respond regarding availability, conflicts, scope and fees.

Professional and medical-legal consulting is entirely separate from patient care. Please do not send patient records until an engagement has been confirmed in writing.

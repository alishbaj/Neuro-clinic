Who this is for

Your most important asset is the one you rarely check.

Executives, entrepreneurs, physicians, attorneys and other professionals often manage every risk except the one inside their own head. Long hours, travel across time zones, disrupted sleep, high stress and deferred medical care add up quietly. This assessment is a structured, confidential review with a neurologist, built around a schedule like yours.

We make no claims about “performance enhancement” or cognitive optimization. What we offer is a rigorous, evidence-based assessment of neurological and vascular risk, and a plan to protect what you have for the long term.

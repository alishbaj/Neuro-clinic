Neurology Care Navigation & Family Support

Neurological illness affects the whole family. We provide independent neurological education and care-navigation support to help families better understand complex neurological conditions and prepare for conversations with their treating medical team.

Book a Family Consultation
How we help

Understanding is the first form of care.

When someone you love has a stroke, a dementia diagnosis, a seizure disorder or an unexplained neurological illness, the hardest part is often not the treatment. It is understanding what is happening, what the options are, and what to ask. A dedicated session with a neurologist, focused on you as the family, can change that.

  • Plain-language explanation of a diagnosis, test results or imaging
  • Help understanding the treatment options that have been offered
  • Preparation for hospital, rehabilitation or clinic conversations, including a written list of questions
  • Guidance on what to watch for and when to seek urgent care
  • Orientation to the kinds of support, services and specialists that may be relevant
  • A second opinion for the patient, when the patient is able to participate and is located in a state where Dr. Al-Habib is licensed

Scope of this service. Education and care-navigation sessions are consultative and educational. They do not establish a physician–patient relationship with your family member, do not replace the care of the treating team, and do not include diagnosis, treatment orders or prescriptions for a person who is not our patient. Where the patient wishes to become a patient of the practice, a separate consultation is arranged. Final scope language is subject to review by the practice’s healthcare attorney.

Welcome

Where prevention comes first

Welcome to Longevity Neurology Center, where we pioneer a preventive approach to adult neurology.

Located in Scottsdale, Arizona, our practice offers individualized care focused on lifestyle, nutrition, and proactive strategies for lasting brain health. Led by Dr. Tasha Powell, appointments are available both in person and via telehealth, designed for patients across the Phoenix Valley and beyond.

About the Center Meet Dr. Powell

A Preventive Neurology System

Comprehensive & preventive neurological care

Four pillars that shape every plan of care we build with you.

Preventive Neurological Assessments

We focus on identifying potential risks and creating personalized strategies to safeguard your long-term brain health before problems arise.

Comprehensive Neurological Care

Dr. Powell offers expert diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing management for a wide array of neurological conditions.

Lifestyle & Nutritional Guidance

We integrate evidence-based lifestyle interventions, including nutrition and exercise, to optimize your neurological function and overall well-being.

Advanced Therapeutic Options

When indicated, we provide advanced treatments such as Botox injections for neuromuscular conditions and headaches, plus trigger point injections and nerve blocks for specific pain syndromes.

Practice Areas

Conditions we care for

Our neurology clinic provides a full range of evaluations, treatment and recommendations, including for neurological conditions such as:

  • Headaches, including migraines
  • Traumatic and non-traumatic acquired brain injuries
  • Impact of menopause and perimenopause on cognition, headaches and stroke
  • Multiple sclerosis and other demyelinating disorders
  • Chronic neck and back pain
  • Stroke and cerebrovascular disease
  • Alzheimer’s disease and dementia
  • Movement disorders, including Parkinson’s disease and tremors
  • Neuropathy
  • Seizures and epilepsy, including vagal nerve stimulator therapy
  • Dizziness, POTS and dysautonomia
  • Identifying and treating people at high risk of neurological disorders based on family history, lifestyle and genetic predisposition

Friendly Reminder

This is a fee-for-service practice. No insurance is billed.

A superbill is provided for each visit, which you may submit to your insurance for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Dr. Powell offers expert consultations — in person and by tele-visit, with no referral needed — within a fee-for-service model. This approach ensures dedicated time for your unique health journey, sooner appointment availability, and a focus on empowering you with strategies for prevention.

Please note

Labs, medications, EMG/nerve conduction studies, EEGs, neuro-cognitive testing and any imaging ordered will be billed to your insurance, as they would be from a conventional doctor’s office.

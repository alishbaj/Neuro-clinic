Our framework

The 7 Pillars of Brain Longevity™

Most of what protects the brain over a lifetime is not exotic. It is a small number of factors, addressed consistently and reviewed by someone who understands how they interact.

01Vascular HealthBlood pressure, cholesterol, glucose and the small vessels of the brain.
02MovementRegular physical activity is one of the best-studied protectors of brain function.
03SleepQuality and duration of sleep, and screening for sleep apnea.
04NutritionDietary patterns with the strongest evidence for vascular and cognitive health.
05Cognitive HealthStaying mentally engaged, hearing health and recognising early change.
06Emotional Well-BeingStress, mood and social connection all shape the brain.
07PreventionScreening, medication review and a plan you can actually follow.

Questions patients ask

Is this practice right for me?

We see adults with neurological symptoms or diagnoses who want more time and a personalized plan; people seeking a second opinion; those concerned about stroke risk or living with migraine; and healthy adults who want an evidence-based approach to protecting their brain. If neurology is not the right fit, we will say so.

Do you take insurance?

No. This is a direct-pay practice, which is what makes longer visits and thorough record review possible. Fees are published on our Pricing page and shown again before you book. We can provide an itemized receipt you may submit to your insurer or HSA/FSA.

Where do I need to be for a virtual visit?

Telehealth licensing is state-based. At the time of your visit you must be physically located in a state where {DOCS} is licensed, currently Texas. This is confirmed when you book.

Can you order tests or prescribe medication?

Yes, where appropriate. Laboratory tests and imaging can be ordered at a facility near you, and most neurology medications can be sent electronically to your pharmacy. Controlled substances follow state-specific rules that we will explain.

Is this a replacement for my current neurologist?

It does not have to be. Many patients use us for a second opinion or a focused brain-health assessment while continuing with their treating team. Others choose ongoing care with us. Either way, we coordinate and communicate clearly.

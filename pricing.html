Pricing questions

Why direct-pay?

Because insurance-based visits are built around volume. Direct-pay lets us schedule 60- and 90-minute visits, review records before you arrive, and write a plan you can actually read. Fees are transparent and shown before you book.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

In most cases, yes. Consultations with a physician are generally eligible expenses. We provide an itemized receipt with the codes your plan may require. Please confirm with your plan administrator.

Can I submit to my insurance for reimbursement?

You may. We are out-of-network with all insurers and do not bill insurance directly. We provide an itemized superbill you can submit; reimbursement depends on your plan and is not guaranteed.

Do you accept Medicare?

Direct-pay arrangements with Medicare beneficiaries are subject to specific federal rules. Please contact us before booking so we can explain the current arrangement. [Counsel to confirm opt-out status and language before launch.]

What is your cancellation policy?

Visits may be rescheduled or cancelled without charge up to 48 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged in full, because the time was reserved and prepared for. [Final policy subject to attorney review.]

What is not included?

Laboratory, imaging and pharmacy costs are billed separately by those facilities and may be covered by your insurance. Professional and medical-legal services are quoted separately.

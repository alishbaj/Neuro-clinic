About Us

Comprehensive & preventive neurological care tailored to you

Welcome to Longevity Neurology Center in Scottsdale, Arizona — a concierge neurology practice offering individualized care focused on lifestyle, nutrition and prevention.

The Center

A fundamental shift in how healthcare is approached

At Longevity Neurology Center, we are moving beyond reactive treatment to proactive prevention and personalized strategies.

Our focus is on early interventions to prevent or delay the onset of chronic neurological diseases, rather than waiting until individuals are already ill. This approach sometimes moves beyond simply following evidence-based guidelines to embrace a holistic perspective, considering individual circumstances and the rapidly evolving scientific understanding of brain health.

What Guides Us

The principles behind every plan of care

{{icon:compass}}

Prevention first

Many neurological conditions have a long prodromal phase. We use that window to act, rather than wait for a diagnosis to force our hand.

{{icon:target}}

Individualized, not templated

Your circumstances, lifestyle, genetic predispositions and risk factors shape a plan built specifically for you.

{{icon:hand-heart}}

Meeting you where you are

Care is tailored to your needs and your capacity — practical steps you can actually sustain, not an unrealistic overhaul.

{{icon:users}}

A direct relationship

Our fee-for-service model restores the traditional doctor–patient relationship: more time, more frequent contact, fewer constraints.

{{icon:leaf}}

Lifestyle as medicine

Nutrition, exercise, sleep and stress management are integrated alongside evidence-based medical treatment — not bolted on afterwards.

{{icon:graduation-cap}}

Education as treatment

We want you to recognise the early signs of neurological disorders and to take an active role in your own brain health.

Our Doctor

Dr. Tasha Powell

Board Certified Neurologist

Dr. Tasha Powell is the visionary founder and CEO of Longevity Neurology Center. With over 20 years of experience in medicine, she is passionate about shifting the focus in neurology towards prevention. Dr. Powell champions strategies that address brain and nervous system health before diseases manifest, drawing on her unique background with the U.S. Military, exposure to traditional wellness practices, and a deep commitment to patient education.

She believes in meeting patients where they are, offering individualized care tailored to their needs and capacity.

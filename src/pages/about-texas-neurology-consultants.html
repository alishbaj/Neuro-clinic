About Us

Independent neurology care, serving DFW since 2003

Texas Neurology Consultants, PLLC is a physician-owned private practice in Plano, Texas, specialising in disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves and muscles.

The Practice

Physician-owned, and intentionally independent

We have been proudly serving communities in DFW and beyond since 2003, with over 40 years of combined physician experience.

Being physician-owned matters more than it sounds. It means the length of your appointment, the tests we order and the treatment we recommend are decided in the exam room by the neurologist who is caring for you — not set by a hospital system or an outside investor. Our neurologists are board certified, and our diagnostic testing is performed and interpreted in our own office.

What Guides Us

The principles behind every plan of care

{{icon:hand-heart}}

Compassionate care from day one

Neurological symptoms are frightening, and the wait for answers is hard. We take that seriously from your very first phone call.

{{icon:clipboard-list}}

Diagnosis before treatment

A careful history and a thorough neurological examination come first. Tests confirm or exclude what the examination suggests — they do not replace it.

{{icon:shield-check}}

Independent judgement

Physician-owned and unaffiliated, so clinical decisions answer to the evidence and to the patient in front of us.

{{icon:microscope}}

Testing in house

EEG, ambulatory EEG, EMG/NCS, sleep studies and cognitive testing happen here, which means fewer referrals out and faster answers back.

{{icon:users}}

Continuity over convenience

You see your neurologist at follow-up, not whoever happens to be free. Your history stays with the physician who knows it.

{{icon:graduation-cap}}

Education as treatment

We explain what we found, what it means and what comes next — in language you can repeat to your family when you get home.

Our Doctor

Alla Al-Habib, M.D.

Triple Board Certified Neurologist

Dr. Al-Habib is board certified by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology in neurology and in vascular/stroke neurology, and UCNS certified in headache medicine. She completed her neurology residency at Baylor College of Medicine and a vascular and stroke fellowship at Washington University in St. Louis.

Before joining Texas Neurology Consultants she worked as a neurohospitalist across the Texas Health Resources and Baylor Scott & White systems, and as a teleneurologist with Mercy Virtual Care Center. She is listed as a Texas Medical Board expert panelist.

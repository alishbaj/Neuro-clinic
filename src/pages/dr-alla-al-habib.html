About the Neurologist

Expert care for the brain, nerves and blood vessels

Alla Al-Habib, M.D. is a triple board-certified neurologist at Texas Neurology Consultants, caring for patients in North Dallas from her office on the Texas Health Plano campus. She has practised in both inpatient and outpatient settings and is listed as a Texas Medical Board expert panelist.

Dr. Al-Habib is board certified by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology in neurology and in vascular/stroke neurology, and is UCNS certified in headache medicine. She has a broad passion for neurology as a whole, with a special interest in stroke and headache medicine — two areas where early, accurate diagnosis changes what is possible for a patient.

She looks forward to connecting with new and current patients at Texas Neurology Consultants and providing the expert care they need.

Professional Journey

Education & training

Dr. Al-Habib earned her medical degree from Jordan University of Science and Technology in Irbid, Jordan. She then relocated to the United States to complete a postdoctoral research fellowship in emergency medicine and an observership at Baylor College of Medicine in Houston, Texas.

She went on to complete her neurology residency at Baylor College of Medicine, followed by a vascular and stroke fellowship at Washington University in St. Louis, Missouri — training that sits behind her work with stroke and cerebrovascular disease today.

Contributing to her breadth of experience, Dr. Al-Habib served as a neurohospitalist in Plano, Texas, covering multiple hospitals across the Texas Health Resources and Baylor Scott & White Health systems. She later served as a teleneurologist with Mercy Virtual Care Center, based in Chesterfield, Missouri, caring for patients at a distance from the bedside.

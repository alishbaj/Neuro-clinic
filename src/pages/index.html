Conditions we care for

Our neurology clinic provides a full range of evaluations, treatment and recommendations, including for neurological conditions such as:

  • {{icon:check}}Headaches, including migraines
  • {{icon:check}}Traumatic and non-traumatic acquired brain injuries
  • {{icon:check}}Impact of menopause and perimenopause on cognition, headaches and stroke
  • {{icon:check}}Multiple sclerosis and other demyelinating disorders
  • {{icon:check}}Chronic neck and back pain
  • {{icon:check}}Stroke and cerebrovascular disease
  • {{icon:check}}Alzheimer’s disease and dementia
  • {{icon:check}}Movement disorders, including Parkinson’s disease and tremors
  • {{icon:check}}Neuropathy
  • {{icon:check}}Seizures and epilepsy, including vagal nerve stimulator therapy
  • {{icon:check}}Dizziness, POTS and dysautonomia
  • {{icon:check}}Identifying and treating people at high risk of neurological disorders based on family history, lifestyle and genetic predisposition

Friendly Reminder

This is a fee-for-service practice. No insurance is billed.

A superbill is provided for each visit, which you may submit to your insurance for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Dr. Powell offers expert consultations — in person and by tele-visit, with no referral needed — within a fee-for-service model. This approach ensures dedicated time for your unique health journey, sooner appointment availability, and a focus on empowering you with strategies for prevention.

{{icon:info}}

Please note

Labs, medications, EMG/nerve conduction studies, EEGs, neuro-cognitive testing and any imaging ordered will be billed to your insurance, as they would be from a conventional doctor’s office.

Meet

Dr. Tasha Powell

Board Certified Neurologist

Dr. Tasha Powell is the visionary founder and CEO of Longevity Neurology Center.

With over 20 years of experience in medicine, she is passionate about shifting the focus in neurology towards prevention. Dr. Powell champions strategies that address brain and nervous system health before diseases manifest, drawing on her unique background with the U.S. Military, exposure to traditional wellness practices, and a deep commitment to patient education. She believes in meeting patients where they are, offering individualized care tailored to their needs and capacity.

No, a referral is not required to schedule an appointment at Longevity Neurology Center.

Preventive neurology focuses on identifying individuals at risk for neurological disorders and implementing strategies — lifestyle, nutrition, early interventions — to prevent or delay the onset of these conditions, rather than just treating them after they appear.

Dr. Powell can order any necessary tests. These tests — labs, imaging, EMG/NCS, EEG and so on — will be performed at external facilities and billed to your insurance by those facilities, just like from any other doctor’s office.

Patients are provided with a detailed superbill after each visit. This document includes the necessary diagnostic and procedure codes and can be submitted to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement.

Please note that reimbursement depends on your individual plan and coverage, and payment for services is due at the time of your appointment.

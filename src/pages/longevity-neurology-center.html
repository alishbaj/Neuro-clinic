Why We Practise This Way

Beyond reactive treatment

At Longevity Neurology Center, we represent a fundamental shift in how healthcare needs to be approached. We are moving beyond reactive treatment to proactive prevention and personalized strategies.

Our focus is on early interventions to prevent or delay the onset of chronic neurological diseases, rather than waiting until individuals are already ill. This approach sometimes moves beyond simply following evidence-based guidelines to embrace a holistic perspective, considering individual circumstances and the rapidly evolving scientific understanding of brain health.

The Model

This is a fee-for-service practice. No insurance is billed.

The direct fee-for-service system is intentionally designed to bring back the traditional model of a direct patient–doctor relationship. This model allows Dr. Powell to dedicate more time to her patients and see them more frequently. This increased interaction is crucial for implementing and adjusting lifestyle measures aimed at optimizing health and longevity.

A Patient-Centered Approach

Direct doctor–patient relationship

Personalized neurological care, built on candour and shared decision-making.

Patients generally appreciate Dr. Powell’s candid and thorough approach. She places significant emphasis on educating patients about their disease process and strongly encourages their active involvement in treatment plan decision-making.

Dr. Powell has cultivated a strong following from her patients due to her passion for disease prevention through nutrition and lifestyle modifications. There is robust evidence supporting the power of these interventions to modulate many common disease processes encountered in our society today.

New Patient Information

What to bring to your first appointment

  • {{icon:check}}A government-issued photo ID.
  • {{icon:check}}Your insurance card and your pharmacy benefit card.
  • {{icon:check}}A physician referral authorization, if your plan requires one.
  • {{icon:check}}A complete list of your medications — prescriptions and over-the-counter — with strength, dose and frequency.
  • {{icon:check}}Recent medical reports: lab results and imaging scans or reports.
  • {{icon:check}}A medical power of attorney, if one applies.
  • {{icon:check}}Your completed patient information packet.
  • {{icon:check}}A list of the questions you want to ask Dr. Al-Habib.
{{icon:clock}}

Arrive early if your paperwork is not finished

If your registration packet is not complete when you arrive, please come 30 to 45 minutes before your appointment time so we can finish it without shortening your visit with the doctor.

Insurance & Billing

We accept most major insurance plans

Including Medicare. Coverage differs plan by plan, so please confirm with us before your visit.

{{icon:credit-card}}

How billing works

Your visit is filed with your insurance in the usual way. Copays, deductibles and coinsurance apply according to your individual plan, and copays are collected at the time of your appointment.

Because networks change, please call us at {{site.phone}} to confirm that we are in network with your specific plan before your first visit.

Some plans require a referral from your primary care physician before they will cover a neurology consultation. Checking this in advance avoids an unexpected bill.

{{icon:alert-circle}}

What we cannot accept

  • Medicaid, and Medicare or Medicaid replacement plans.
  • Patients whose visit relates to a motor vehicle accident, a workplace incident, or an active lawsuit — unless the reason for the visit is unrelated to that injury or claim.

If you are unsure whether this applies to you, call the office before booking and we will work it out with you.

Medical Records

Requesting your records

Medical records releases for Texas Neurology Consultants are processed through HealthMark Group, our records partner.

To request a copy of your records, or to have them sent to another provider, contact HealthMark Group directly on 1-800-659-4035. You will need to complete a signed authorisation before records can be released.

If you need records sent to us from a previous provider, let our front office know at the time of scheduling — having them in hand before your visit makes the appointment far more useful.

The portal is not for emergencies

If you are having stroke symptoms — sudden weakness or numbness on one side, trouble speaking or understanding, sudden vision loss, severe dizziness or a sudden severe headache — call 911 immediately. Do not message the office and do not drive yourself.

Insurance & Pricing Information

No insurance accepted

Longevity Neurology Center operates on a direct fee-for-service model.

{{icon:credit-card}}

How payment works

We do not accept or bill insurance for Dr. Powell’s consultation services, and full payment is required at the time of the visit.

However, any imaging, laboratory testing, procedures or medications ordered by Dr. Powell are billed to your insurance just as they would be at any traditional clinic, subject to your plan’s usual copays, deductibles and coverage rules.

This approach allows Dr. Powell to provide a higher level of personalized care, spend more dedicated time with each patient, and offer faster, more accessible appointments.

{{icon:file-text}}

Superbills & reimbursement

A superbill will be provided for each visit, which you may submit to your insurance for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

You may request an itemized receipt after your visit, which you can then submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement if you have out-of-network benefits.

Please note that Medicare and Medicaid do not allow any filing for reimbursement for services provided under this model.

{{icon:alert-circle}}

Medicare patients

Our clinic operates on a fee-for-service concierge model and does not participate with Medicare or Medicaid. While we are happy to evaluate and care for you, services provided through our clinic are not eligible for reimbursement through government-sponsored insurance programmes.

Please email us at {{site.email}} for any questions or assistance.

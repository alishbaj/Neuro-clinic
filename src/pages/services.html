Services & Diagnostics

Diagnosis, testing and treatment — under one roof

Conditions We Treat

Adult neurology, across the board

Dr. Al-Habib evaluates and treats disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves and muscles, with particular depth in stroke and headache medicine.

{{icon:heart-pulse}}

Stroke & Cerebrovascular Disease

Evaluation after a stroke or TIA, investigation of the underlying cause, and secondary prevention — blood pressure, cholesterol, atrial fibrillation, diabetes and antiplatelet or anticoagulant therapy. Dr. Al-Habib is fellowship trained and board certified in vascular neurology.

{{icon:activity}}

Headaches & Migraines

Diagnosis of migraine, tension-type, cluster and secondary headaches; acute and preventive medication strategies; and botulinum toxin (BOTOX®) injection for chronic migraine. Dr. Al-Habib is UCNS certified in headache medicine.

{{icon:brain}}

Epilepsy & Seizure Disorders

Working out whether an episode was a seizure, identifying the seizure type with EEG, starting and adjusting anti-seizure medication, and counselling on driving, work and safety.

{{icon:droplet}}

Neuropathy & Nerve Pain

Numbness, tingling, burning and weakness in the hands and feet. We use EMG and nerve conduction studies to locate the problem and look for the treatable causes behind it.

{{icon:hand-heart}}

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Confirmation by nerve conduction study, grading of severity, and guidance on splinting, injection or surgical referral where it is warranted.

{{icon:clipboard-list}}

Memory Concerns

Structured assessment of memory and thinking, including NeuroTrax cognitive testing, to separate normal ageing from mild cognitive impairment, dementia and the reversible causes that can mimic both.

{{icon:compass}}

Parkinson’s Disease

Diagnosis and long-term management of Parkinson’s, including medication timing, motor fluctuations and the non-motor symptoms that often matter most day to day.

{{icon:target}}

Tremor & Movement Disorders

Essential tremor, dystonia, tics and other movement disorders — distinguishing between them and treating accordingly, including botulinum toxin where indicated.

{{icon:shield-check}}

Multiple Sclerosis

Diagnosis and management of MS and other demyelinating disease, including disease-modifying therapy, relapse management and symptom control.

Diagnostics

Testing performed in our office

Because these studies are done and interpreted here, results come back sooner and your neurologist reads them in the context of your examination.

{{icon:activity}}

EEG (Electroencephalogram)

A painless recording of the brain’s electrical activity through small electrodes placed on the scalp. An EEG helps us identify seizure activity, characterise a seizure type, and investigate episodes of altered awareness or unexplained spells.

Typically 45–60 minutes. No needles, no radiation.

{{img:consult-exam|A neurologist performing a bedside neurological examination||(max-width: 900px) 92vw, 480px|lazy}}
{{icon:clock}}

Ambulatory EEG

The same recording, carried out over one or more days while you go about your normal routine at home. Events that are infrequent or happen mostly at night are far more likely to be captured this way than during a short in-office study.

{{icon:moon}}

Sleep Study

Investigation of sleep apnoea, excessive daytime sleepiness, restless legs and other sleep disorders. Untreated sleep apnoea worsens stroke risk, headache frequency, seizure control and memory, which is why it matters so much in neurology.

{{icon:activity}}

EMG & Nerve Conduction Studies

Two paired tests that measure how well nerves conduct signals and how muscles respond. They locate the site of a nerve problem — carpal tunnel, a pinched nerve in the neck or back, or a more widespread neuropathy — and grade how severe it is.

{{icon:clipboard-list}}

NeuroTrax Cognitive Testing

A computerised assessment of memory, attention, processing speed, executive function and motor skills, scored against age-matched norms. It gives an objective baseline that can be repeated over time to show whether something is genuinely changing.

Medication Management

Starting, adjusting and monitoring long-term neurological therapy — anti-seizure medication, migraine preventives, disease-modifying therapy for MS, Parkinson’s medication and stroke prevention regimens — with attention to interactions and side effects.

How It Works

From first visit to a plan

Most patients are seen, tested and started on a plan within a few weeks.

Throughout, Dr. Al-Habib will explain what each test is for and what the results mean, so that you can take an informed part in the decisions about your own treatment.

{{icon:info}}

About imaging

MRI and CT scans are arranged at an outside imaging facility and billed to your insurance by that facility, as they would be from any other doctor’s office. Testing performed in our office — EEG, EMG/NCS, sleep studies and cognitive testing — is billed by us.

  1. Consultation & Examination

    A full neurological history and hands-on examination, plus a review of your medications and any prior imaging, labs or records you bring with you.

  2. Targeted Testing

    Where the examination points to it, we arrange the specific studies needed — EEG, EMG/NCS, a sleep study, cognitive testing, blood work or imaging — rather than a broad sweep of tests ordered in case something turns up.

  3. Diagnosis & Treatment Plan

    We go through the results together, agree on a treatment plan, and set a follow-up interval so we can tell whether it is working and adjust it if it is not.

Our Treatment Process

A collaborative journey

We believe in a transparent and collaborative approach to your care.

Throughout every step of this process, a preventive neurologist educates you about brain health and promotes awareness of the early signs and symptoms of neurological disorders.

{{icon:info}}

Direct fee for service

A system designed to bring back the traditional model of the direct patient–doctor relationship. It allows Dr. Powell to spend more time with her patients, and see them more frequently, so that she can implement lifestyle measures to optimize health and longevity.

For any labs, medications and imaging ordered, those will be billed to insurance as from any other doctor’s office.

  1. Comprehensive Evaluation & Examination

    A thorough review of your medical history, current symptoms, lifestyle factors, and any prior workup, imaging or labs you provide. Based on this initial assessment, Dr. Powell will determine whether you are a candidate for our concierge membership or can be seen on an as-needed basis.

  2. Further Diagnostics & Record Gathering

    If necessary, additional workup — specialized labs, imaging or neurocognitive testing — will be ordered. We will also make efforts to obtain any pertinent prior medical records to ensure a complete picture of your health.

  3. Personalized Therapy Initiation & Goal Setting

    Together we will initiate a therapy plan, highly individualized to your diagnosis, identified risk factors and personal health goals. We will discuss whether the focus will be a traditional evidence-based medical approach, a lifestyle, nutrition and supplement-focused approach, or a complementary approach integrating both.

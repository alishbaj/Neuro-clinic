SMS Messaging Opt-In Policy

1. Introduction

Texas Neurology Consultants offers SMS messaging services to keep you informed about our latest products, services, promotions and updates. By opting in to receive SMS messages from Texas Neurology Consultants, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Opt-in process

To opt in to receive SMS messages from Texas Neurology Consultants, you can:

  • Text us at (972) 403-3100.
  • Sign up through our website by providing your mobile number and consenting to receive SMS messages.
  • Opt in verbally during the account set-up process.

3. Consent

By opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from Texas Neurology Consultants at the mobile number you provided. These messages may include promotional content, service updates and other relevant information. Message frequency may vary.

4. Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply to SMS messages sent by Texas Neurology Consultants. Please check with your mobile carrier for details on your plan’s rates and charges.

5. Opt-out process, or if you need help

You can opt out of receiving SMS messages from Texas Neurology Consultants at any time by:

  • Texting “STOP” to our number.
  • Contacting our customer service team for assistance.
  • Texting HELP to reach us for assistance.

Upon opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages from Texas Neurology Consultants, except for messages necessary to confirm your opt-out request.

6. Privacy

Texas Neurology Consultants respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. Your mobile number and any other personal information provided will be used solely for the purpose of sending SMS messages as described in this policy. For more information, please review our Privacy Policy.

Mobile information privacy: your mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Data protection

Your data will not be transferred to external organizations under any circumstances. We maintain strict policies and safeguards to prevent unauthorized sharing of your information.

8. Changes to this policy

Texas Neurology Consultants may update this SMS Messaging Opt-In Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website. Your continued use of our SMS messaging services after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
